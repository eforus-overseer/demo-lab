# Artifact provenance

Gallery UI and browser teaching examples were created for this collection. Original artifacts retain the authorship and licensing of their source repositories; this gallery does not relicense them. Each displayed artifact links to the original.

The Visual Vocabulary relationship taxonomy credits the Financial Times Visual Journalism Team and the Graphic Continuum. Its 62 example SVGs are the original project’s invented-data examples. The original games retain their source behavior and external dependencies. No new stock photography is included.
